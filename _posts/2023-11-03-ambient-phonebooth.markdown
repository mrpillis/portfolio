**AmbientPhoneBooth**  
*D. Pillis*
*MIT, USA — MIT Media Lab / MIT BCS / MIT Museum / MIT Innovation Initiative*<br>
[Project Page](https://www.dpillis.com/ambient-phonebooth) / [Publication](https://www.scribd.com/document/797644250/Human-MaterialInteractionforExpression-Performance-andRemembering)
<blockquote>
  <p>
We rebuild the classic “ambient room” interaction system on a smaller scale, set inside a phone booth. AmbientPhoneBooth was an interactive audio installation that transformed a phone booth into an on-demand spatial audio experience. The system captured auditory memories and recreated spatialized soundscapes with delayed feedback, evoking nostalgia and a sense of temporal presence. Users dial a series of numbers to cue a vibrotactile sensory immersive sound experience related to a memory of the past, evoking a sense of 'being together' with someone in a past environment.
  </p>
</blockquote>
