**Ancestral Simulations**  
*D. Pillis, Georine Pierre, City Science Group, MIT Media Lab*<br><br>
[Project Page](https://www.dpillis.com/ancestral-simulations) / [arXiv](https://www-prod.media.mit.edu/projects/ancestral-storytelling-movement-and-memory/overview/)
<blockquote>
  <p>
This interactive motion capture project, in collaboration with Georine Pierre, used ancestral storytelling and Haitian folklore dance to explore “ancestor simulations.” Participants engaged with recorded rituals via a Kinect Azure, layering movement and memory over time. The evolving archive connected cultural history, water symbolism, and embodied interaction within a responsive installation.
  </p>
</blockquote>
