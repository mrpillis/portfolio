**IDEO Emerging Technologies**  
*D. Pillis, MIT, USA — MIT Media Lab / MIT BCS / MIT Museum / MIT Innovation Initiative*
[Project Page](https://www.dpillis.com/) / [arXiv](#)
<blockquote> <p>This series of tests explores how immersive office design impacts cognitive and emotional states. Participants were prompted to read in varying postures—seated, reclined, and in transition—within dynamic, projection-mapped interiors developed at IDEO. Lighting, visuals, and acoustic elements adapt in real-time to user interaction. The project investigates how spatial affordances influence neural activity, comfort, and focus. This research bridges spatial computing, wellness technology, and embodied interaction for applications in health, workplace design, and extended reality.
 </p> </blockquote>
