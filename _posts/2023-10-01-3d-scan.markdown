<blockquote> <p>A series of 3D scans of huan subjects. The Lenscloud scanner captures high-resolution 360-degree scans of human bodies and large objects using 120 synchronized cameras. It produces two sets of images per scan—one for geometry and one for texture—and allows quick review of captured data within minutes. The system is reconfigurable to fit various assets and supports applications in AR/VR, digital fabrication, and visualization. Processing is done via RealityCapture in the Immersion Lab or with external software. Users can export 3D models into standard formats for use in tools like Blender, Maya, or 3ds Max, enabling fast and flexible creation of detailed photogrammetric assets.
