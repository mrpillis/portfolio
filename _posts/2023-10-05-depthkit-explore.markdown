**Media Lab Memory Project**  
*D. Pillis*
*MIT, USA — MIT Media Lab*<br><br>
[Project Page](https://www.dpillis.com/media-lab-metaverse) / [arXiv](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGqn4WvEW8/UZoEQhZdme36zJW_QPMjzQ/view?utm_content=DAGqn4WvEW8&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link2&utm_source=uniquelinks&utlId=h7fa136c356)
<blockquote> <p>The Media Lab Memory Project is an online and augmented reality exhibition designed to document and visualize the history of the MIT Media Lab. Built on an interactive three.js model of the E14 and E15 buildings, the system serves as a portal for storing and mapping alumni memories, media, and archival materials throughout the physical space of the Lab. Developed in anticipation of the Lab’s 40th anniversary in 2025, the project functions as a social repository and augmented reality archive, integrating historical collections from contributors including Will Glistens, Joost Bonsen, Rosalind Picard, and Jon Ferguson, among others.
 </p> </blockquote>
