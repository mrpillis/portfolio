This project integrates real-time Stable Diffusion into TouchDesigner for dynamic virtual production. Using a high-resolution LED wall, we explore how generative AI can transform set design, props, and storytelling when imagery is synthesized live in response to performers and camera.

Our case studies show how generative pipelines streamline production workflows, enabling rapid, cost-effective prototyping of environments for in-camera visual effects and filmmaking, and point toward room-scale generative displays in the spirit of Sutherland's “ultimate display.” The work received the Best Presentation award at the 2025 MIT Generative AI for Design Workshop.
