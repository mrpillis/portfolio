We describe Robot Cinematography, a system in which a Boston Dynamics Spot quadruped operates as a spatially tracked camera platform inside a real-time generative virtual production volume. Spot carries a RED Komodo-X cinema camera tracked with stYpe RedSpy; its position and orientation drive real-time Stable Diffusion rendered to an LED wall through TouchDesigner, so the generative backdrop responds continuously to the robot's movement.

The work treats the robot not as a motion-control rig executing pre-planned moves but as a creative partner whose locomotion, gait, and gaze become cinematographic choices. We report on the integration of robot telemetry with the virtual production pipeline, the latency budget required for in-camera visual effects, and early observations from staging scenes with human performers alongside the quadruped.
