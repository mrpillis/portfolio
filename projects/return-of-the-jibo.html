This paper explores the integration of Jibo, a social robot, with generative AI in film-making education. Deployed in college classrooms, Jibo supported creative workflows by helping students write scripts, storyboard, and act in AI-generated films. Using tools like Scratch and real-time rendering systems, students engaged with Jibo as both a character and a collaborator.

The study documents increased student engagement, emotional bonding, and reduced anxiety, suggesting that social robots can enhance AI-assisted learning in creative disciplines. By embodying AI in a personable form, Jibo bridged technical tools with human-centered pedagogy, highlighting new possibilities for interactive storytelling and robot-enhanced virtual production education.
